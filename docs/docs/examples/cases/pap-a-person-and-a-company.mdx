# A Person and a Company

<CaseDeck id="pap-a-person-and-a-company" />

Ada's agent finds Manzanita Outfitters by its domain, asks about her Ridge Jacket
before anybody signed in, and is told to sign in. Ada signs in on Manzanita's own
page, allows "See your orders", and the company answers.

What each step uses, in the example flows:

- Reading what the company publishes: [Company discovery](/examples/company-discovery).
- Proving who the agent is, signed out: [Session JWT](/examples/session-jwt) and [Session lifecycle](/examples/session-lifecycle).
- The company asking for a sign-in, then signing in: [Conversation control](/examples/conversation-control) and [Device Sign-In](/examples/device-sign-in).

[A user with a company](/examples/user-with-a-company) runs this case and
the [next one](/examples/cases/pap-approve-once) as one journey, on the wire, both
sides from the SDK. There the agent is called Atlas.
