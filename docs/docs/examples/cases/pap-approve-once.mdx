# A Change, Approved Once

<CaseDeck id="pap-approve-once" />

Ada's jacket isn't warm enough. Manzanita doesn't exchange it yet: it returns an
operation, its exact terms. Her agent shows them to her, and sends the same
request again only with her approval of that revision. The approval says that
Ada gave it herself: at Poppy 1.0 every approval names its approver, the person
or her agent on an instruction she gave it, and the company may decline one her
agent gave.
