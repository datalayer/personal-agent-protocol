# Two Companies Together

<CaseDeck id="pap-two-companies" />

Ledgerly, an accounting firm, is a company to Ada's agent, Atlas, and a personal
agent at her bank, Northwind. There is no company-to-company protocol: two
ordinary relationships meet in Ledgerly. Every payment the bank returns goes to
Ada, the first and the next, and she can't ask for that to stop.

The [Two companies](/examples/two-companies) example flow is this case on the
wire, with all three parties running in one process.
